Lab manual

The lab manual documents how we get things done as a team in our lab. It is also an open window into our culture for those interested in joining us.

About

The intention of creating this document came before the lab was even formed. It is adapted from the great Computer-Oriented Geoscience Lab manual. Even though we share a lot of our principles and we were inspired by their design and philosophy, a straight copy would not be appropriate (even if we count that their license allows it). Additionally, our lab is being built from scratch, so we will slowly test and adapt the practices and procedures that work for our research and context.

Beyond that, we can expect that when the lab grows, the potential for conflicts and misunderstandings might increase as well. Thus, the ultimate goal for this “bureaucracy”: to help to decrease the likelihood of conflicts and misunderstandings, creating a more pleasant and productive environment for everyone.

Following our main reference, our manual is also divided into two sections:

  • Handbook: Which describes the team principles and work ethic.
  • Guides: The practical guides involving the day-to-day work in the lab.

To help develop and improve the manual, the team members are expected to
carefully read it and provide feedback, suggestions, and modifications. To that end, check out the Contributing Guide for this website.

Did you enjoy what you see and also want to join the team?

Technical skills can be learned, coding can be mastered and research experience can be gained, but culture is built together and requires shared values. If you want to join or collaborate with use, please first ask yourself why. Explore our handbook to see if what we expect when doing science aligns with your own views and values. After that, you can see how to join us here.
