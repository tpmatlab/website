Health and well-being

Your first priority should always be your own health, safety, and well-being. No project, paper, grant, or collaboration is more important than that.

Health resources in São Carlos

  • Hospital Universitário: The UFSCar hospital is excellent, has a walk-in service and provides free medical care for the whole city. It is close to the UFSCar campus and is open 24h a day.
  • DeEsp: The university sports department is full of free activities that you can sign up for and has a large range of pools and sports courts that can also be used. The UFSCar campus also has multiple climbing spots and a climbing wall that is free to use.
  • Ministério da Saúde - Suicídio (Prevenção): The ministry of health website has resources for suicide prevention and how to get help with mental health issues.
  • Centro de Valorização da Vida: Offers a 24h help line by phone, chat, email, and in-person (the closest center to campus is in Pinheiros).

Entertainment in São Carlos

São Paulo is a massive city and a huge cultural center in Brazil. There are many things to do, often for free, across the city. Here are a few tips.

  • Buses are free on Sundays. You can take as many as you’d like and they often won’t be as crowded as on weekdays.
  • Parque Ecológico de São Carlos is a free park with a zoo, it is a great place to visit some amazing animals and have a picnic. It is also neighboring UFSCar campus.
  • Centro de Divulgação de Astronomia is a free observatory and astronomy center that is open to the public on weekend nights and at special astronomical events.
  • SESC São Carlos hosts art exhibits, plays and concerts. The exhibits are usually free. They also have sports courts and many free activities for children.
  • Parque do Kartódromo is a free park with dedicated areas for walking and running. It is also a nice place to picnic, meet pets, and enjoy the outdoors. It is located in Cidade Jardim, a neighborhood next to USP Campus.
