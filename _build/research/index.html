Our research

If something happens, it means it changes…

And we want to be there to see it!

Better yet, we will understand it, predict it, and control it!

That is our endgoal.

To achieve it, we aim to combine the world-leading large-scale research facilities which enable in-situ and advanced experiments to validate and calibrate our computational models, solving real-world problems.
