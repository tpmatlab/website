## Health resources in São Carlos

* [Hospital Universitário](https://www.gov.br/hubrasil/pt-br/hospitais-universitarios/regiao-sudeste/hu-ufscar): 
  The UFSCar hospital is excellent, has a walk-in service and provides free
  medical care for the whole city. It is close to the UFSCar campus and is open
  24h a day.
* [DeEsp](https://cepe.usp.br/): The university sports department is full of
  free activities that you can sign up for and has a large range of pools and
  sports courts that can also be used. The UFSCar campus also has multiple
  climbing spots and a climbing wall that is free to use.
* [Ministério da Saúde - Suicídio (Prevenção)](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/assuntos/saude-de-a-a-z/s/suicidio-prevencao):
  The ministry of health website has resources for suicide prevention and how
  to get help with mental health issues.
* [Centro de Valorização da Vida](https://cvv.org.br/): Offers a 24h help line by phone, chat, email, and in-person (the closest center to campus is in [Pinheiros](https://www.google.com/maps/place/R.+Cerro+Cor%C3%A1,+792+-+21+-+Vila+Romana,+S%C3%A3o+Paulo+-+SP,+05061-100/@-23.5401302,-46.7049922,19.29z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x94ce57d3e451c08b:0x28663176a7d2cad5!8m2!3d-23.5397673!4d-46.7047116?entry=ttu)).

## Entertainment in São Carlos

São Paulo is a massive city and a huge cultural center in Brazil. There are
many things to do, often for free, across the city.
Here are a few tips.

* Buses are free on Sundays. You can take as many as you'd like and they often
  won't be as crowded as on weekdays.
* [Parque Ecológico de São Carlos](https://www.instagram.com/parqueecologicosaocarlos/)
  is a free park with a zoo, it is a great place to visit some amazing animals
  and have a picnic. It is also neighboring UFSCar campus.
* [Centro de Divulgação de Astronomia](https://cdcc.usp.br/observatorio/) is a 
  free observatory and astronomy center that is open to the public on weekend
  nights and at special astronomical events.
* [SESC São Carlos](https://www.sescsp.org.br/) hosts art exhibits, plays and
  concerts. The exhibits are usually free. They also have sports courts and many
  free activities for children.
* [Parque do Kartódromo](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Z4r9D4K2zTdUj21T8) is a free
  park with dedicated areas for walking and running. It is also a nice place to 
  picnic, meet pets, and enjoy the outdoors. It is located in Cidade Jardim, a
  neighborhood next to USP Campus.
